02Ongoing Projects

Room-Temperature NV-Photonic Quantum Node

NV-centre photonics · Analog front-end · Hardware-Informed AI
BDQuantum

A single quantum node based on nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres in diamond — a spin qubit operating at room temperature, removing the cryogenic requirement of superconducting and trapped-ion platforms. The project pairs a from-scratch optical/microwave bench with a differentiable photonic design loop, extending prior simulation work on a dual-NV solid-core photonic crystal fibre (PCF).

Tier 1CW-ODMR Bench (<€250)

Optically detected magnetic resonance via HPHT high-NV⁻ microdiamond, 532 nm excitation, BPW34/OPA140 photodiode detection, and an ADF4351 microwave synthesizer driving a PCB loop antenna at the 2.87 GHz zero-field splitting. Targets fluorescence readout, ODMR contrast, and Zeeman splitting.

Tier 2Pulsed Control

Microcontroller-sequenced Rabi, Ramsey, and Hahn-echo protocols for qubit initialisation, gate operation, and T₂ coherence characterisation, using a fast RF switch and ~20 dBm amplifier stage.

Tier 3Hardware-Informed Design Loop

Measured ODMR contrast and T₂ feed calibration of a 100-layer PCF model; autodiff inverse design optimises the fibre cross-section beyond the current 3.04% core power fraction (A_eff = 0.2135 µm²), co-optimising photonic confinement against quantum-node performance.

Silica PCF Photon Collector for NV Centres

A silica photonic crystal fibre designed to collect 637 nm NV-centre zero-phonon-line (ZPL) photons — an optical interconnect for a room-temperature quantum node.
